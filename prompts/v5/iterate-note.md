## Key changes

- Added a prominent human-referent gate for people-focused subjects.
- Kept ethnic and religious subject IDs unchanged; added `ukrainian_` to other
  domestically scoped IDs.
- Added `ukrainian_people_with_mobilization_deferment` and renamed
  `migrant_workers` to `migrant_workers_in_ukraine`.
- Allowed separate records with the same `subject_id` for independently
  portrayed people or subgroups.
- Required non-empty `mention_terms`, `portrayal`, and `evidence`.
- Clarified Russian-state metonymy, GUR attribution, subject-local stance, and
  approval of violence against a subject.
- Consolidated repeated instructions. The shared prompt decreased from 1,062
  to 762 words, a 28% reduction.
